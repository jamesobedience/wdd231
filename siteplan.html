Website Project Plan

Project: Simple Business Ideas

Site Name

Simple Business Ideas

The name “Simple Business Ideas” was selected because it clearly communicates the purpose of the website. It focuses on practical and easy-to-start business opportunities for beginners.

Optional Domain: simplebusinessideas.com

Site Purpose

The website provides simple business ideas for individuals who want to start small businesses. It also offers basic tips on how to manage finances and attract customers.

Scenarios

  • What business can I start with little money?
  • How can I successfully manage a small business?
